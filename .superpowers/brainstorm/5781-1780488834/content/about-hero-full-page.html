About Otix

Otix is a media tracking and discovery platform. Track movies, shows, games, and music in one place — rate what you've experienced, write reviews, build curated lists, and earn XP as you go.

Connect your accounts from Steam, Plex, Spotify, YouTube, TMDb, and Google to import your libraries, sync your history, and share what you're playing or listening to.

Profile Scores

XP & Level

Your activity score

Every action on Otix earns XP — writing reviews, rating media, building lists, completing workouts, maintaining streaks, and more. Each level requires 300 XP.

1–4Novice
5–9Apprentice
10–19Adept
20–34Expert

Aura

0–1000 scale, starts at 100

Your aura is a composite score that represents your standing in the community. It's built from four pillars: contribution, trust, tenure, and impact.

100–149Newcomer
150–249Contributor
250–399Trusted

User Rating

1–10 scale from other users

Other users can rate your profile on a 1–10 scale. Your average rating directly boosts or reduces your aura through the trust pillar.
