What is Otix?

🎬 Movies 📺 Shows 🎮 Games 🎵 Music

Otix is a media tracking and discovery platform. Track movies, shows, games, and music in one place — rate what you've experienced, write reviews, build curated lists, and earn XP as you go.

Connect your accounts from Steam, Plex, Spotify, YouTube, TMDb, and Google to import your libraries, sync your history, and share what you're playing or listening to. Your profile becomes a living record of your taste across every medium.
